# Ravi beat sheet (v2)

Ravi is the viewer's stand-in: he *lives* the problem while the narrator explains. Two presentation modes:
- **Full-frame** (cold open, chapter transitions, finale): Ravi large, centre or left third.
- **Host corner** (inside modules): Ravi at ~40% size in the bottom-left or bottom-right third, reacting on cue phrases while the graphics run. Scenes reserve that third (layouts shift ~300 px).

Cue phrases are exact narration words (`cueFrame`). Moods/poses refer to `src/character` (`MOODS`, `POSES`).

| Where | Cue phrase → Ravi |
|---|---|
| **Cold open** (full) | "Forty calls a day" → phone buzzing in hand, `worried`; "police are coming" → `shocked`, phone to ear; "stop. Take a breath" → lowers phone, `sad`→`neutral`, big breath (shrug); "what the law really says" → looks at camera, `explaining`; "my own case" → small knowing smile, `relieved`, points to camera |

Lip-sync only when the line is framed as Ravi speaking (cold open close, M10 comment CTA, M13 teaser); otherwise he reacts silently.
